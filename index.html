01 THE APPROACH

Less “let the AI do it.”
More “let’s do this well.”

AI can move quickly. Good engineering still takes direction, context, and a human who knows what “done” should mean. Learn a repeatable way to bring those pieces together.

↓

02 THE FIELD GUIDE

A workflow you
can trust.

Nine focused practices for the complete journey: understand the work, make a plan, build in context, and learn from the result.

01
/scope

Start with a clear ask

Turn the broad request into a small, verifiable next step. Give the agent a useful target instead of a blank page.

CLARITY↗
02
/audit

Get to know the codebase

Gather the project’s real structure, commands, and conventions so future work is grounded in how the team actually builds.

CONTEXT↗
03
/architect

Make the shape visible

Choose the boundaries, data flow, and files before implementation. A deliberate plan makes the work easier to review.

INTENT↗
04
/develop

Build in the right context

Work through the plan in focused increments and keep the project’s existing patterns in view as code takes shape.

CRAFT↗
05
/check

Read the change critically

Inspect the diff for edge cases, regressions, and mismatches with the brief. Treat the first answer as a draft.

JUDGMENT↗
06
/test

Prove what should work

Choose checks that exercise the requested behavior. Use results to improve the implementation, not just to decorate it.

EVIDENCE↗
07
/document

Leave useful context behind

Capture the decisions and operating details that will help the next person pick up the work without starting over.

CONTINUITY↗
08
/sync

Make the handoff easy

Bring the branch, review, and summary together so teammates can understand the change and decide what happens next.

COLLABORATION↗
09
/debug

Follow the failure to its cause

Reproduce the problem, trace the behavior, and address the underlying issue—then protect the fix with a regression check.

RESILIENCE↗

A practical sequence, not a rigid recipe. Apply the steps that serve the work.
