04 FIELD NOTES

A few things
worth knowing.

A quick orientation before you get started.

01Is this for engineers who are new to AI coding tools?+

Yes. Start with the core workflow and bring whichever AI coding assistant you already have access to. The emphasis is on transferable engineering habits, not one vendor's interface.

02Is the course about writing prompts?+

Prompts are one part of the picture. The course also covers scoping work, reading a codebase, planning a change, checking implementation, testing behavior, and making a clear handoff.

03Does the workflow apply to existing projects?+

That's a central use case. The practices include learning a project's conventions before making changes and tracing failures back to their cause rather than patching symptoms.

04Do I need a specific AI provider or framework?+

No specific provider is required by this field guide. Adapt the workflow to the tools, language, and review process you use on your own projects.
